The hardware behind the system

We build every installation around equipment we trust to hold up on the Lahore grid, year after year.

Inverter

GOODWE 8kW hybrid inverter

The inverter is the brain of the system — converting the DC power your panels generate into usable AC, and deciding moment to moment whether your home runs on solar, battery, or grid.

Storage

Lithium battery storage

Stackable lithium modules bank your excess solar generation during the day, so evenings and load-shedding hours draw on stored power instead of the grid.
